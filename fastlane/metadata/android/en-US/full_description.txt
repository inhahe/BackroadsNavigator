Turn-by-turn car navigation that can keep you off the highways. Maps are OpenStreetMap, place search is OpenStreetMap's Nominatim, and routes come from OpenRouteService.

Features:

• Backroads mode: routes avoid highways and toll roads and prefer the shortest way over the fastest, so you see the smaller roads.
• Exclude streets: long-press a street on the map to keep routes off it, and manage the list of excluded streets.
• Search for an address or place and route there.
• Turn-by-turn navigation with spoken directions, remaining distance, arrival time and a list of all the directions.
• Rerouting from where you are when you leave the route.
• North-up or heading-up map while navigating.
• Saved places: Home, Work and favourites.
• No Google Play Services in the app: your position comes from Android's own location service (GPS, plus network location where the phone has it).

Routing needs a free OpenRouteService API key: sign up at account.heigit.org, create a key, and paste it into the app when it asks. The free plan allows 2,000 routes a day.

No ads, no tracking.
